# 🎵 Spurtify

**🇺🇸 English (US)** | [🇮🇹 Italiano](./README.it.md) | [🇷🇺 Русский](./README.ru.md) | [🇨🇳 简体中文](./README.zh-CN.md)

**Spurtify** is a lightweight, fast, and modern desktop music player for Windows, designed to manage, download, and play your entire music library in one clean, distraction-free interface.

</div>

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **YouTube Search & Download:** Search for tracks directly inside the app or paste a video link to save high-quality audio tracks to your local library.
* **Offline Music Library:** Import entire local music folders from your PC and instantly filter tracks by title or artist.
* **Playlist Management:** Create custom playlists, add or remove songs with a single click, and play your collections seamlessly.
* **Full-Featured Player:** Always-accessible bottom dock with Shuffle, Repeat (single track or queue), interactive seek bar, and dedicated volume control.
* **Modern Dark Interface:** High-contrast dark theme crafted for visual comfort and snappy desktop responsiveness.

---

## 📥 Download & Installation (Windows)

1. Go to the **[Releases](../../releases)** section on the right side of this page.
2. Download the **`Spurtify-Setup-1.1.0.exe`** installer.
3. Run the downloaded file and follow the setup wizard (a Desktop shortcut will be created automatically).

> **Windows SmartScreen Note:** Because this is an independent application not distributed through the Microsoft Store, Windows may show a protection prompt on first launch. Click **More info** → **Run anyway** to proceed with the installation.

---

## 🖥️ System Requirements

* **Operating System:** Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
* **Internet Connection:** Required only for searching and downloading new tracks; library and playlist playback works 100% offline.
